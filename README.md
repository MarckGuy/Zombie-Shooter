<h1>Zombie Shooter</h1>
Zombie Shooter is a game where you shoot zombies. Zombies chase you. Zombies hurt you. Zombies bad. Shoot zombies with gun. Gun use bullets. you have many bullets.
Zombies die. Zombies get you points. You may like points. You may not like points. You get points until you die. You lose points when you die. Dying is bad. No bullets is bad.
Losing points is bad. Makes you sad. Zombies make you sad. Don't die :)
